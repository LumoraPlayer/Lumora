# Lumora UI/UX Design Review (October 2026)

**Scope & Methodology:** Independent read-only audit of Lumora's Android Views/XML interface across all 7 real 1080p TV screenshots (`docs/screenshots/*.png`), all 32 XML layouts (`res/layout*/`), drawables/animators (`res/drawable/`, `res/animator/`), multi-tier dimensions/styles (`res/values/`, `res/values-sw540dp/`, `res/values-sw540dp-w1280dp/`, `res/values-port/`), 26 localized string sets, and the Kotlin focus/adapter layer (`com.lumora.adapter.*`, `MainActivity*.kt`, `com.lumora.ui.*`, `com.lumora.auto.*`), graded against the `apk-ui-design` rubric. All 7 screenshots were inspected directly as images.

---

## 1. Top 5 Simplifications

1. **Unify Settings onto standard focus-ring tokens and remove the redundant global `Cancel` / `Save` footer.** Right now, Settings is the biggest source of focus ambiguity in the app (`docs/screenshots/settings-providers.png`, `settings-qr.png`, `plugins.png`). Provider rows (`item_iptv_provider_row.xml`) and installed-plugin rows (`item_plugin_row.xml`) use the static `<shape>` `@drawable/bg_category_panel` instead of a focus-state `<selector>`, so focused rows have no white `@color/focus` outline at all—only a subtle 1.06x scale. Meanwhile, the Settings left nav rail (`nav_rail_item_glass.xml`, `nav_rail_item_background.xml`) puts `state_selected="true"` ahead of `state_focused="true"` with no combined state and no `@color/focus` stroke, making a focused+selected nav item visually indistinguishable from an unfocused one. Replacing those backgrounds with `@drawable/card_surface_background` / `@drawable/bg_select_item` and removing the persistent bottom `Cancel` / `Save` bar (which is a no-op on 15 of the 16 auto-saving panes and duplicates the provider form's own inline controls) immediately fixes focus visibility and reclaims vertical space across all of Settings.

2. **Simplify provider rows in Settings by moving per-provider content gates (`TV` / `Movies` / `Series`) into the Edit form.** `item_iptv_provider_row.xml` currently packs up to 7 interactive controls (`[Enabled CheckBox] [Name/URL] [TV] [Movies] [Series] [Browse] [Edit] [Remove]`) into a single horizontal card narrowed by `settings_content_inset` (`200dp` on TV, leaving ~553dp total card width). Creating a 6-stop horizontal D-pad chain inside every list row makes vertical traversal and lateral escape back to the Settings nav rail error-prone, and in verbose locales (German, Russian) the right-hand controls consume ~450–520dp, crushing the provider name and URL. Keeping the list row to a single primary action (toggle/open, plus `Browse` on shares and `Edit` / `Remove`) and moving the `TV` / `Movies` / `Series` checkboxes into `settingsIptvFormSection` eliminates both the D-pad obstacle course and the i18n text crush.

3. **Streamline shelf headers (`item_shelf.xml`) and make the top shelf's header reachable—or fold `See All` into the shelf row.** On Series and Movies (`docs/screenshots/series.png`, `films.png`), every shelf header carries three separate focusable controls (`SEE ALL`, `☆`, `✕`), yet `ShelfPosterAdapter` (`ShelfAdapter.kt:163-178`) intercepts `DPAD_UP` on every poster in the top shelf (`Newest (30)`) and jumps straight to the top tab bar—bypassing shelf 0's `SEE ALL`, `☆`, and `✕` buttons completely. Furthermore, on the Home screen (`MainActivity.kt:1186-1191`), `homeShelfAdapter` never wires `onSeeAllClick`, so every Home shelf displays a focusable `SEE ALL` button that does nothing when pressed. Hiding `shelfSeeAllButton` when no handler is supplied, moving category pin/hide exclusively to the sidebar/long-press menu (where it already lives), and wiring explicit D-pad UP/DOWN between shelf headers and poster rows removes dead buttons and makes `See All` reliably reachable.

4. **Give the Live TV preview pane a dedicated header/split slot or one-press hide instead of floating it over focusable EPG cells.** In `docs/screenshots/live-tv.png`, the `260x136dp` `livePreviewGutter` floats over the top-right corner of `liveContent`, directly occluding the programme blocks of the top 4–5 channels while the time ruler (`16:41`, `17:11`, `17:41`) continues above it. Although `updateGuideRowWrap()` attempts to reserve `marginEnd` on overlapped rows, it does not run in `submitList`'s commit callback, does not inset `guideHeaderScroll`, and still allows horizontally scrolled EPG cells to pass underneath the opaque video box. Docking the preview in a clean top header band (beside current programme synopsis) or collapsing the EPG timeline to a single "Now / Next" column while the preview is active eliminates both the occlusion bug and the heavy per-row `HorizontalScrollView` sync on low-end Fire TV sticks.

5. **Centralize recycled-view focus-transform reset and debounced image loading into a shared ViewHolder helper.** The `apk-ui-design` rubric highlights stuck `focus_scale` transforms (`scaleX`/`scaleY`/`translationZ`) and un-cancelled async image loads as the most common adapter bugs. Currently, only 2 of the 10 adapters (`LiveGuideAdapter` and `EpisodeAdapter`) reset `scaleX`/`scaleY`/`translationZ` on `bind()`, and only 2 (`LiveGuideAdapter` and `SportsAdapter`) debounce and cancel per-ViewHolder image jobs on rebind. Extracting a 15-line `View.resetFocusScale()` + debounced per-holder `Job` helper and calling it across `PosterGridAdapter`, `ShelfPosterAdapter`, `CategoryAdapter`, `SportsAdapter`, `CatchupAdapter`, `SideMenuCategoryAdapter`, `SearchResultsAdapter`, and `DownloadAdapter` eliminates stuck enlarged cards and coroutine churn during fast D-pad scrolling.

## 2. Findings

### Pass 1: Focus & Navigation (Top Priority)

#### [F-01] `P0` — Provider rows, plugin rows, and selected Settings nav rail items have no visible focus ring
- **Screen:** Settings (Providers, Plugins, and left Navigation Rail)
- **Evidence:** `docs/screenshots/settings-providers.png`, `docs/screenshots/settings-qr.png`, `docs/screenshots/plugins.png`; `app/src/main/res/layout/item_iptv_provider_row.xml:13`; `app/src/main/res/layout/item_plugin_row.xml:11`; `app/src/main/res/drawable/bg_category_panel.xml:3-8`; `app/src/main/res/drawable/nav_rail_item_glass.xml:4-33`; `app/src/main/res/drawable/nav_rail_item_background.xml:4-15`; `app/src/main/java/com/lumora/MainActivitySettings.kt:2118`.
- **What's wrong:**
  1. `item_iptv_provider_row.xml` (`@+id/rowRoot`) and `item_plugin_row.xml` (`@+id/pluginRow`) set `android:background="@drawable/bg_category_panel"`, which is a static `<shape>` with a `1dp @color/color_border` (`#14FFFFFF`) stroke and no `state_focused` state. In `settings-providers.png`, D-pad focus is on the `ip` provider row: it is scaled 1.06x (`@animator/focus_scale`), slightly overlapping the `Jellyfin` row below it, but has **no white focus border and no fill change**.
  2. In `nav_rail_item_glass.xml` and `nav_rail_item_background.xml` (used by all Settings nav rows in `activity_settings.xml:65-174` and `item_plugin_nav_row.xml:11`), `<item android:state_selected="true">` is placed before `<item android:state_focused="true">`, with no `<item android:state_selected="true" android:state_focused="true">` entry and no `@color/focus` stroke in any state. Because Android's `StateListDrawable` matches the first applicable item, whenever D-pad focus is on the currently selected nav rail item (which `selectSection()` requests at `MainActivitySettings.kt:2118`), the Focused state is completely shadowed by the Selected state.
- **Why it matters on a 10-foot D-pad UI:** Viewers sitting across the room cannot tell whether D-pad focus is in the left Settings rail or on a provider/plugin row in the right pane. In `settings-providers.png` and `plugins.png`, nothing on the entire screen has a white `@color/focus` outline.
- **Suggested fix:**
  1. Change `android:background` on `rowRoot` in `item_iptv_provider_row.xml:13` and `pluginRow` in `item_plugin_row.xml:11` from `@drawable/bg_category_panel` to `@drawable/card_surface_background` (which already defines a `2dp @color/focus` stroke and `@color/surface_emphasis` fill on `state_focused="true"`).
  2. In `nav_rail_item_glass.xml` and `nav_rail_item_background.xml`, add a top `<item android:state_selected="true" android:state_focused="true">` state and a `2dp @color/focus` stroke on both focused states (matching `@drawable/bg_select_item`).

#### [F-02] `P0` — Search overlay (`dialog_search.xml`) D-pad UP/DOWN bypasses the `All | Live | Films | Series` filter chips
- **Screen:** Global Search overlay
- **Evidence:** `app/src/main/res/layout/dialog_search.xml:221-305`; `app/src/main/java/com/lumora/MainActivitySearch.kt:115-127`; `app/src/main/java/com/lumora/adapter/SearchResultsAdapter.kt:118-131`; `app/src/main/java/com/lumora/ui/OnScreenKeyboard.kt:153-178`.
- **What's wrong:** In `dialog_search.xml`, `searchFiltersRow` (`searchFilterAll`, `searchFilterLive`, `searchFilterFilms`, `searchFilterSeries`) sits directly above `searchResults` in the right column. However:
  - `MainActivitySearch.kt:116` sets `resultsAdapter.topRowFocusUpTargetId = R.id.searchKeyboard` instead of targeting the active filter chip in `searchFiltersRow`. Pressing `DPAD_UP` from the top row of search results jumps over the filter chips and teleports focus into the on-screen keyboard on the left.
  - `MainActivitySearch.kt:126` sets `keyboard.bottomRowDownTarget = resultsList`, jumping over both `searchRecentsBlock` and `searchFiltersRow`.
  - None of the 4 filter chips in `dialog_search.xml:232-294` have `OnKeyListener` or `nextFocusDown` / `nextFocusLeft` / `nextFocusUp` wiring to enter `searchResults` on `DPAD_DOWN` or return to `searchKeyboard` on `DPAD_LEFT`.
- **Why it matters on a 10-foot D-pad UI:** A viewer who types a query and moves into the results grid cannot press `UP` to filter by `Live`, `Films`, or `Series` (unlike the main Discover screen, where `discoverGridFocusUpTargetId()` in `MainActivityDiscover.kt:407-415` specifically routes `UP` from the top row of the grid to the active filter chip). Reaching the filter chips requires guessing a geometric `RIGHT` move from the top rows of the keyboard. *(Runtime verification recommended to confirm which keyboard keys geometrically align with `searchFiltersRow`.)*
- **Suggested fix:** Mirror `MainActivityDiscover.kt:407-415`: point `resultsAdapter.topRowFocusUpTargetId` at the currently selected filter chip when `searchFiltersRow.isShown` (falling back to `R.id.searchKeyboard`), and add a `View.OnKeyListener` on the 4 filter chips so `DPAD_DOWN` focuses item 0 of `searchResults`, `DPAD_LEFT` on `searchFilterAll` focuses `searchKeyboard`, and `DPAD_UP` focuses `searchCloseButton`.

#### [F-03] `P1` — Top shelf's `SEE ALL`, `☆`, and `✕` buttons are skipped on `DPAD_UP` from posters, trapped on `DPAD_UP`, and `SEE ALL` is a dead button on Home
- **Screen:** Series, Movies, and Home shelves
- **Evidence:** `docs/screenshots/series.png`, `docs/screenshots/films.png`; `app/src/main/res/layout/item_shelf.xml:31-77`; `app/src/main/java/com/lumora/adapter/ShelfAdapter.kt:38, 81-86, 163-178`; `app/src/main/java/com/lumora/MainActivity.kt:1186-1191`.
- **What's wrong:**
  1. In `ShelfPosterAdapter` (`ShelfAdapter.kt:163-178`), pressing `DPAD_UP` on any poster in the top shelf (`bindingAdapterPosition == 0`, e.g., `"Newest (30)"` in `series.png` and `films.png`) unconditionally intercepts the event and focuses `homeSearchBar` or `topRowFocusUpTargetId` (the active tab in the top bar). Even when focus is on the 4th or 5th poster directly beneath shelf 0's `SEE ALL`, `☆`, and `✕` controls, `DPAD_UP` skips right over them into the tab bar.
  2. Conversely, `shelfSeeAllButton`, `shelfPinButton`, and `shelfHideButton` (`ShelfAdapter.kt:81-86`) have no `DPAD_UP` listener to escape the outer `RecyclerView` (`seriesContent` / `filmsContent` / `homeContent`). Per `apk-ui-design` Failure Mode #1 (`RecyclerView.focusSearch()` scopes `FocusFinder` to itself as root), pressing `DPAD_UP` while focused on shelf 0's `SEE ALL`, `☆`, or `✕` fails to cross out of the `RecyclerView` into the tab bar.
  3. In `MainActivity.kt:1186-1191`, `homeShelfAdapter` does not pass `onSeeAllClick` (leaving the default `{}` no-op), yet `shelfSeeAllButton` in `item_shelf.xml:32` is unconditionally `VISIBLE` and `focusable="true"`. Every shelf on the Home tab renders a prominent `SEE ALL` pill that does nothing when clicked.
- **Why it matters on a 10-foot D-pad UI:** Users cannot naturally reach `SEE ALL` on the first shelf by pressing `UP` from its posters, get stuck if they reach shelf 0's header from the sidebar and press `UP`, and hit dead `SEE ALL` buttons on every Home shelf.
- **Suggested fix:**
  1. Pass `showSeeAllButton: Boolean = true` (or make `onSeeAllClick: ((ContentShelf) -> Unit)? = null`) in `ShelfAdapter` and set `seeAllButton.visibility = if (onSeeAllClick != null) View.VISIBLE else View.GONE`.
  2. Add an `OnKeyListener` on `seeAllButton`, `pinButton`, and `hideButton` in `ShelfViewHolder` so `DPAD_UP` at `bindingAdapterPosition == 0` escapes to `homeSearchBar` / `topRowFocusUpTargetId`, and `DPAD_DOWN` focuses the first visible poster in `itemsList`.
  3. In `ShelfPosterAdapter`, let `DPAD_UP` at `isTopShelfProvider()` land on `seeAllButton` (when visible) before escaping to the tab bar on a second `DPAD_UP`.

#### [F-04] `P1` — Settings leaves background browse tabs visible and focusable, and shows a misleading `Cancel` / `Save` footer on auto-saving panes
- **Screen:** Settings
- **Evidence:** `docs/screenshots/settings-providers.png`, `docs/screenshots/settings-qr.png`, `docs/screenshots/plugins.png`; `app/src/main/res/layout/activity_settings.xml:2623-2672`; `app/src/main/java/com/lumora/MainActivitySettings.kt:2241-2245, 2327-2336`.
- **What's wrong:**
  1. Unlike `showSearchDialog()` (`MainActivitySearch.kt:238`) and `showDiscoverSearchOverlay()` (`MainActivityDiscover.kt:200`), which hide `binding.tabBar` while the overlay is open, `showProviderSettings()` (`MainActivitySettings.kt:2241-2245`) leaves the top browse tab bar visible and focusable. In `settings-providers.png`, `settings-qr.png`, and `plugins.png`, the `"Discover"` tab still shows its active underline above Settings, and pressing `DPAD_UP` from the top of Settings moves focus out of Settings into the browse tabs (where pressing `OK` on a tab abruptly closes Settings).
  2. `settingsFooter` (`activity_settings.xml:2623-2672`) renders a permanent bottom bar with `Cancel` and `Save` across all 16 Settings sections. As documented in `MainActivitySettings.kt:2328-2336`, every pane except the Add/Edit Provider sub-form persists changes immediately on click, so `Save` and `Cancel` do the exact same thing (`activeSettingsOverlay?.dismiss()`). Meanwhile, `settingsIptvFormSection` already has its own inline `Cancel` button (`settingsIptvFormCancel`, `activity_settings.xml:1252`).
- **Why it matters on a 10-foot D-pad UI:** Keeping the browse tab bar active above Settings creates an accidental exit trap on `DPAD_UP`, and a persistent `Cancel` / `Save` footer falsely implies that toggling checkboxes in General, Playback, Filters, or Streaming Sites (~70 rows) can be reverted with `Cancel` or requires scrolling to the bottom to press `Save`.
- **Suggested fix:** Hide `binding.tabBar` (or disable focus on browse tabs) while `activeSettingsOverlay != null`, and move `settingsCancelButton` / `settingsSaveButton` inside `settingsIptvFormSection` (the only place where staged form fields exist) so the other 15 panes reclaim the footer's vertical height.

#### [F-05] `P1` — Live TV guide (`LiveGuideAdapter`) lacks explicit `DPAD_UP` escape at row 0 and requires two `DPAD_LEFT` presses to reach the Category Sidebar
- **Screen:** Live TV (EPG Guide)
- **Evidence:** `docs/screenshots/live-tv.png`; `app/src/main/res/layout/item_guide_channel_row.xml:15-53`; `app/src/main/java/com/lumora/adapter/LiveGuideAdapter.kt:184-195`.
- **What's wrong:**
  1. Every other main list/grid adapter in the codebase (`CategoryAdapter`, `PosterGridAdapter`, `ShelfAdapter`, `SportsAdapter`, `CatchupAdapter`, `SearchResultsAdapter`, `DownloadAdapter`) intercepts `KEYCODE_DPAD_UP` at `bindingAdapterPosition == 0` and explicitly calls `requestFocus()` on `topRowFocusUpTargetId` (`tabLive`, etc.), because `RecyclerView.focusSearch()` scopes `FocusFinder` to itself as root (`apk-ui-design` Failure Mode #1). `LiveGuideAdapter` has **no** `KEYCODE_DPAD_UP` handler on `channelInfo`, `favStar`, or the EPG programme blocks (`makeBlock()`).
  2. Unlike `CategoryAdapter` (which hides `categoryStar` until focused), `item_guide_channel_row.xml:36` keeps `guideChannelFav` (`☆`) permanently `VISIBLE` on every row, and `LiveGuideAdapter.kt:184-189` intercepts `KEYCODE_DPAD_LEFT` on `channelInfo` to focus `favStar`. Moving from the channel list back to `categorySidebar` therefore requires two `LEFT` presses on every channel row, and on the second `LEFT` press from `favStar`, there is no explicit handler to focus `categorySidebar`'s selected row (or `sidebarExpandButton` when the rail is collapsed).
- **Why it matters on a 10-foot D-pad UI:** Moving between the Category Sidebar, the Channel column, and the top `Live TV` tab is the highest-frequency navigation loop in the app. Requiring an extra stop on every row's `☆` button doubles the `LEFT` keystrokes to switch categories, and relying on default focus search out of `liveContent` risks dead keys or geometric jumps.
- **Suggested fix:** Add `var topRowFocusUpTargetId: Int = R.id.tabLive` and `var leftEdgeFocusTarget: (() -> Boolean)? = null` to `LiveGuideAdapter`, handle `KEYCODE_DPAD_UP` at `bindingAdapterPosition == 0` across `channelInfo`, `favStar`, and `makeBlock()`, and consider Either hiding `favStar` unless the row is focused or keeping long-press `OK` as the primary favourite gesture so a single `LEFT` from `channelInfo` returns directly to the Category Sidebar.

#### [F-06] `P1` — Content Detail screen has broken D-pad links between Play/Action buttons, Version chips, and Season chips
- **Screen:** Movie & Series Detail (`contentDetailLayout`)
- **Evidence:** `app/src/main/res/layout/activity_main.xml:1863-2103`; `app/src/main/java/com/lumora/MainActivityLists.kt:798-816, 1298-1335, 1448-1467`; `app/src/main/java/com/lumora/MainActivityCast.kt:39, 81-91`; `app/src/main/java/com/lumora/MainActivity.kt:2412-2450`.
- **What's wrong:**
  1. When a series has both multiple provider versions (`versionsScroll` visible) and seasons (`seasonScroll` visible), `MainActivityLists.kt:813` sets `playButton.nextFocusDownId = firstChip.id` (the first version chip), and each version chip sets `chip.nextFocusUpId = playButton.id` (`line 804`), **but** version chips have no `nextFocusDownId` (or `DPAD_DOWN` listener) pointing to `selectedSeasonChip`, and season chips (`lines 1298-1333`) have no `nextFocusUpId` pointing back to `versionsRow` or `playButton` and no `DPAD_DOWN` listener to focus row 0 of `detailItemsList`.
  2. Only `detailPlayButton` receives `nextFocusUpId = firstCell.id` (when cast faces exist, `MainActivityCast.kt:90`) or `nextFocusDownId`. Its sibling buttons in the same horizontal row (`detailFavoriteButton`, `detailDownloadButton`, `detailFindStreamButton`, `detailTrailerButton`) have no `nextFocusUpId` or `nextFocusDownId` set. When `castMembers` is empty, `detailPlayButton.nextFocusUpId` is reset to `View.NO_ID` (`MainActivityCast.kt:39`), leaving no explicit `UP` link to `detailBackButton` (which sits outside the `ScrollView`).
- **Why it matters on a 10-foot D-pad UI:** Because `contentDetailLayout` is a `ScrollView` containing multiple `HorizontalScrollView`s (`detailCastScroll`, `detailVersionsScroll`, `detailSeasonScroll`) and a `FullHeightRecyclerView` (`detailItemsList` — Failure Mode #2 and #3 in `apk-ui-design`), unlinked `UP`/`DOWN` transitions rely on geometric `FocusFinder` across scrolled horizontal strips, which can skip over the active season chip or fail to reach `detailBackButton`.
- **Suggested fix:** Wire explicit vertical focus transitions across the detail stack: Action buttons (`Play`, `Favorite`, `Download`, `Find Stream`, `Trailer`) ↕ `detailCastRow` (or `detailBackButton` when cast is hidden), Action buttons ↕ `detailVersionsRow` (when visible) ↕ `selectedSeasonChip` ↕ row 0 of `detailItemsList`.

### Pass 2: Visual Consistency with the Design System

#### [F-08] `P1` — 8 of 10 RecyclerView adapters omit `focus_scale` transform resets on `bind()`, and 6 launch un-debounced, un-cancelled image coroutines
- **Screen:** Series, Movies, Home, Discover, Live TV Category Sidebar, Sports, Catch Up, Player Side Menu, Search, Downloads
- **Evidence:** `app/src/main/java/com/lumora/adapter/PosterGridAdapter.kt:185-225`; `app/src/main/java/com/lumora/adapter/ShelfAdapter.kt:181-203`; `app/src/main/java/com/lumora/adapter/CategoryAdapter.kt:134-185`; `app/src/main/java/com/lumora/adapter/SportsAdapter.kt:438-548`; `app/src/main/java/com/lumora/adapter/CatchupAdapter.kt:121-135`; `app/src/main/java/com/lumora/adapter/SideMenuCategoryAdapter.kt:128-149`; `app/src/main/java/com/lumora/adapter/SearchResultsAdapter.kt:185-250`; `app/src/main/java/com/lumora/adapter/DownloadAdapter.kt:90-129`.
- **What's wrong:**
  1. Per `apk-ui-design` ("Recycled-view hygiene"), any adapter whose item layout uses `@animator/focus_scale` (`scaleX/Y = 1.06`, `translationZ = 12`) must reset `scaleX = 1f`, `scaleY = 1f`, and `translationZ = 0f` on every `bind()`, because fast RecyclerView recycling interrupts the unfocus animation and leaves stuck transforms on the reused `itemView`. Only `LiveGuideAdapter.kt:239-243` and `EpisodeAdapter.kt:208-213` do this; the other 8 adapters do not.
  2. `PosterGridAdapter`, `ShelfPosterAdapter`, `EpisodeAdapter`, `SearchResultsAdapter` (both `MediaHolder` and `EpgHolder`), and `DownloadAdapter` launch `scope.launch { PosterLoader.fetch(url) }` on `bind()` without storing/cancelling a per-`ViewHolder` `Job` and without a `LOAD_DEBOUNCE_MS` delay.
- **Why it matters on a 10-foot D-pad UI:** Holding down a D-pad direction key scrolls through dozens of posters or categories per second; recycled views retain partial `1.06x` scale and `12px` Z-elevation (rendering crooked or oversized while unfocused) and queue hundreds of wasted image fetches on budget TV sticks.
- **Suggested fix:** Add a shared extension `fun View.resetFocusTransform()` (guarding `if (!isFocused)` with `animate().cancel(); scaleX = 1f; scaleY = 1f; translationZ = 0f`) and call it at the top of `bind()` in all 8 adapters; track `private var imageJob: Job? = null` per `ViewHolder`, cancel it before each bind, and debounce uncached network fetches by `150–200ms`.

#### [F-09] `P1` — Missing `clipChildren="false"` / `clipToPadding="false"` crops the 1.06x focus ring in Settings panes, Player controls, and Discover search
- **Screen:** Settings, Player Controls Overlay, Discover, Category Sidebar
- **Evidence:** `app/src/main/res/layout/activity_settings.xml:25, 44, 50, 706, 732, 794, 861, 906, 996, 1073, 1129, 1270, 1427, 1465, 1514, 2284, 2624`; `app/src/main/res/layout/activity_main.xml:983, 1155, 2539, 2669, 2677`.
- **What's wrong:** `apk-ui-design` notes that any view using `@animator/focus_scale` or `@animator/focus_scale_flat` needs `clipChildren="false"` and `clipToPadding="false"` on itself and its ancestors up to a padded container. Several containers still default to `true`:
  - **Player Controls (`activity_main.xml:2539, 2669, 2677`):** The transport row (`line 2539`, height set by `46dp` `btnPlayPause`) and `playerTrackButtons` + its parent `HorizontalScrollView` (`lines 2669-2681`, height set by `30dp` buttons) do not set `clipChildren="false"` / `clipToPadding="false"`, slicing the top and bottom of the focused button's `2dp–3dp` `@color/focus` ring when scaled to 1.06x.
  - **Discover Search Field wrapper (`activity_main.xml:983`):** The `LinearLayout` wrapping `discoverSearchField` (`lines 983-990`) is `wrap_content` in height with `clipChildren="true"`, clipping the top and bottom focus border of `discoverSearchField` when it scales to 1.06x on TV.
  - **Settings (`activity_settings.xml`):** `settingsNavRail` (`line 25`) and its inner `ScrollView` / `LinearLayout` (`lines 44, 50`), `panePlayback` (`line 1270`), `panePrivacy` (`line 1427`), `paneBackup` (`line 1465`), `paneEpg` (`line 1514`), `paneAbout` (`line 2284`), `settingsFooter` (`line 2624`), and the provider form field groups (`settingsM3uGroup`, `settingsXtreamGroup`, `settingsStalkerGroup`, `settingsJellyfinGroup`, `settingsPlexGroup`, `settingsSmbGroup`, `settingsWebdavGroup`) all omit `clipChildren="false"` / `clipToPadding="false"`.
- **Why it matters on a 10-foot D-pad UI:** Cropped focus borders make focused pills look flat/squared-off on their top, bottom, or side edges—the exact visual defect called out in `apk-ui-design`.

## 3. What's Already Good (Do Not Regress)

1. **Views/XML Architecture & Device-Tier Scaling (`values-sw540dp`):** Keeping the UI 100% Android Views/XML (no Compose) and keying the large-screen resource tier on `values-sw540dp` rather than `uiMode=television` is a smart, pragmatic engineering choice—it guarantees that non-certified Android TV boxes reporting `uiMode=normal` on a 1080p HDMI output still get TV typography and layout metrics.
2. **On-Screen Search Keyboard & Split Results Layout (`OnScreenKeyboard.kt`, `dialog_search.xml`):** Suppressing the full-screen system IME on TV in favor of a custom left-hand `OnScreenKeyboard` with live right-hand search results, starting initial focus on the central `"G"` key (`firstKey()`), and passing the source `Rect` on `DPAD_LEFT` from column 0 of the grid so focus lands on the adjacent right-hand key (`focusLeftTarget`) is exemplary 10-foot D-pad ergonomics.
3. **Deliberate Cross-Boundary D-pad Engineering (`CategoryAdapter`, `SportsAdapter`, `CatchupAdapter`, `EpisodeAdapter`):** The codebase shows deep awareness of `RecyclerView.focusSearch()`'s failure modes: `CategoryAdapter.moveDown()/moveUp()` prevents focus from escaping the bottom of the category rail into the channel list, `MainActivity.dispatchKeyEvent` walks `detailItemsList` by adapter position so `FullHeightRecyclerView` inside a `ScrollView` never jumps over rows, and `MainActivityCast` / `hideContentDetail` restore focus to the exact cast face or poster tile the user came from on `Back`.
4. **Two-Stage Player Controls & Blind Live-TV Surfing (`MainActivity.kt:2395-2403, 2607-2612`):** Using the first `OK` press in fullscreen playback to reveal the controls overlay without pausing the stream, reserving `DPAD_UP`/`DOWN` for instant channel-surfing while controls are hidden, and sliding out the translucent `playerSideMenu` drawer on `DPAD_LEFT` over live video gives Lumora a fast, TiviMate-grade live viewing flow.
5. **Debounced EPG & Logo Loading in `LiveGuideAdapter`:** `LiveGuideAdapter`'s 200ms `LOAD_DEBOUNCE_MS` delay, `BlockState` view-reuse pool in `programRow`, single reusable `GradientDrawable` for channel initials, and `CancellationException`-safe `EpgListCache` in-flight tracking are well-engineered for smooth scrolling through multi-thousand-channel playlists.
